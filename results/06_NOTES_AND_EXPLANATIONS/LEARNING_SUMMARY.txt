WHAT THIS PROJECT TAUGHT

Important concepts covered by this project:
- motion planning
- geometric vs kinodynamic planning
- state representation [x,y,vx,vy]
- RRT tree expansion
- parent-pointer path reconstruction
- collision checking along an edge
- sample-conditioned LQR-CBF basic RRT as an expert teacher
- conditional GANs
- generator and discriminator roles
- generator noise and candidate diversity
- supervised pretraining vs adversarial fine tuning
- final discriminator classifier training
- PyTorch tensors, modules, losses, optimizers, training loops, and checkpoints
- diagnostics for finding bottlenecks
- why position accuracy and full-state accuracy differ
- why honest train/validation/test separation matters
