REAL WORLD IMPACT

Robots need to plan safe motion through obstacles while respecting physical motion limits. Pure random search can waste samples, and pure neural prediction can be unsafe.

This project combines both approaches. The GAN proposes expert-like motions learned from strict sample-conditioned LQR-CBF basic-RRT expansions. The RRT planner checks every proposal with full-edge collision and kinodynamic constraints.

Potential applications include mobile robots, robot arms, warehouse robots, drones, search-and-rescue robots, and space rovers.
